Privacy Policy

Hermes Arthur is a private personal assistant app operated by Adam Gualtieri for his own use only. It is not available to the public and has no other users.

The app accesses Adam's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) solely to read, organise and draft items on his behalf. Data is processed only to perform those tasks, is stored only on Adam's own private systems, and is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Adam can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
